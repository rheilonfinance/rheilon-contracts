# Security

## Reporting a problem

Please report anything that could put funds at risk privately, through this repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability**). Don't open a public issue for it.

Include the contract and function, what can go wrong, and the steps or a test that shows it. You'll get a reply
within a few days.

## Scope

The contracts in `contracts/` as deployed on Robinhood Chain (addresses in the README). Contracts in
`contracts/mocks/` are test-only and never deployed.

## What's known

Findings from the self-review, including the ones that can't be changed in deployed contracts, are listed in
[`docs/security-review.md`](docs/security-review.md). There has been no external audit yet.
